# Operations

## Daily

1. Scheduler selects the next calendar item.
2. AI creates draft content.
3. QA checks privacy, medical claims, advertising language and platform fit.
4. Draft is routed for human approval.
5. Approved content is sent to the configured publishing adapter.
6. Result is logged.

## Weekly

Review reach, saves, shares, profile visits, link clicks and sales. Keep winning topics, create variations, and reduce low-performing themes.

## Safety

Never publish identifiable patient information. Do not use AI output as diagnosis or individualized medical advice. Review clinical claims, statistics, treatment details and local advertising requirements before publication.
